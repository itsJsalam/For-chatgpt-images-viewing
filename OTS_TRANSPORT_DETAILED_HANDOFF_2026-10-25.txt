2. PRODUCT PURPOSE
OTS Transport is a scheduled intercity ride / carpool / seat-booking Android app, initially focused on Khuzdar, Balochistan, with routes to destinations across Pakistan. Currency context: PKR. It should look premium, polished and trustworthy, comparable in quality to established ride-booking apps. User wants an actual Android APK, not a website, and prefers free-first/PKR 0 solutions.

Vehicle types in scope/planning: cars, vans, coasters, buses. Seat maps must eventually vary by vehicle type. Current car requirement is 5 total seats: 1 driver + 1 front passenger + 3 rear seats. Passenger must never be able to select the driver seat.

Important correction: ONE driver account is linked to ONE vehicle for current scope. Do not implement multiple vehicles per driver now; the user may reconsider later.

Drivers must be approved by the owner/authorized admin before they can publish/start operating trips. This must ultimately be enforced by backend permissions, not only a UI state.

App 1 contains passenger and driver modes. App 2 is separate and intended for owner/authorized staff use. Never expose admin-only operations to ordinary passengers/drivers.

Payments (cash, JazzCash, Easypaisa) have been shown/discussed as UI/future features but are not verified as integrated. Do not claim payments, bookings, tickets, seat holds, approvals, SOS, live trip tracking, or notifications are real unless the backend confirms them. The user wants to defer features that require paid services. Do not expose credentials or secrets.
